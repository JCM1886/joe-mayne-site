Carbon Reduction and Feasibility Tool (CRaFT)

A tool that stays with an organisation for its whole climate journey — from target-setting to net-zero and beyond — informing decision-making and building confidence in its climate actions. Content, structure and go-to-market messaging developed during my nearly seven years leading marketing for EcoAct / SE Advisory Services.

What it is
CRaFT
hero screenshot

As businesses work towards net-zero by 2050, robust science-based targets give a clearly defined pathway to reduce carbon emissions. Whether an organisation has already set climate targets or is planning to, CRaFT helps find the right pathway to its climate ambitions — combining historical performance data with emissions-reduction initiatives to forecast, track and realise emissions reductions.

It builds confidence in near- and long-term climate targets by letting an organisation build and compare custom-made, costed emission-reduction scenarios.

How CRaFT helps forecast and track emissions reductions

Forecast your emissions

Houses all emissions-related data and produces visual outputs of how emissions are projected to track into the future.

Set robust climate targets

Visually maps emissions data and reduction scenarios against a science-aligned target, helping identify the best route to hit it and stay on track.

Track your progress

Gives a clear picture of emissions reductions throughout the net-zero journey — add new initiatives, update business-unit performance and build new reduction scenarios.

Engage stakeholders

Breaks a target down internally by business area, so colleagues can be engaged and incentivised to plan and track their own contribution.

Add transparency to disclosures

Helps demonstrate ambitious targets, organisational oversight and genuine emissions-reduction progress to stakeholders.

Key questions on CRaFT

What is CRaFT?

With CRaFT, an organisation can confidently forecast its emissions scenarios, set robust reduction targets and track progress — visualising climate targets and strategically selecting decarbonisation levers for maximum impact.

How does CRaFT help hit reduction targets?

With carbon data and reduction initiatives entered, you can evaluate how different emissions-reduction initiatives affect the short, medium and long-term emissions trajectory, and compare potential initiatives side by side to assess effectiveness and cost simultaneously.

How do you get started with CRaFT?

Getting started typically began with a short demo of the tool. From there, an organisation could choose to have its team set up and trained to run the tool themselves, or have the advisory team manage the platform on their behalf.
