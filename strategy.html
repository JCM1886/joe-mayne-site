EcoAct and Atos

Go-to-Market strategy, stakeholder feedback and enhancement

Add an image or video for this case study

We created the A to Zero go-to-market strategy, which was incredibly successful and gave us the opportunity to organise and represent our complete range of tools and services in a way that was clear, well-structured and accessible. This go-to-market approach ran successfully for several years. However, over that time we ran consultation sessions with our advisory and sales teams to further enhance the offer, and the feedback showed that A to Zero was too linear a programme. To encourage repeat business and ongoing client relationships — and to reflect circularity as a key topic in corporate sustainability, and the fact that sustainability isn't a checkbox you tick once but an ongoing task — we moved to the ACTOR model. This also reflected a shift in focus: the era of assessment, footprints and target-setting was behind us, and the priority now was looking forward to action and implementation. That's where the ACTOR model came in.
