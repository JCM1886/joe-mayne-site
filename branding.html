Brand Evolution

From independent brand to SE Advisory Services

Through the years at EcoAct, we went from an independent start up/scale-up to being acquired by one of our clients then sold to another and finally re-branded as SE Advisory Services to merge into the Schneider Electric brand family.

This meant I gained a lot of experience with brand hierarchy and across a range of different branding disciplines, as well as honing my ability to develop a brand to meet different stakeholder requirements and balance often competing narratives.
