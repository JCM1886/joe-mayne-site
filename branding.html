Branding

Brand and creative work for EcoAct & SE Advisory Services

Report and factsheet design, campaign creative, website rebrands and digital tool content for Schneider Electric's sustainability advisory business. Eleven creative pieces below — covers and descriptions are being added as originals are sourced.

A note on the images on this page: save each cover image into its matching folder under assets/images/branding/<project>/ using the filename referenced in this page's HTML, and the placeholder will be replaced by the real image with no other changes needed.

Digital tools

As lead marketer for our Climate Data Analytics (CDA) tools, I worked cross-functionally with developers, sales and advisory teams to shape the product roadmap and go-to-market strategy. I unified a fragmented toolset into a single, coherent suite, renaming each tool to reflect its actual purpose and to enhance its performance in SERPs. I designed a shared iconographic system to visually unite them, laying the groundwork for full platform integration. However, this work was paused when Schneider Electric acquired SE Advisory Services, pending a combined software strategy.
