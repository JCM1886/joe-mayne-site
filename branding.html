Digital Tools

Digital Tools

Climate Data Analytics

As lead marketer for our Climate Data Analytics (CDA) tools, I worked cross-functionally with developers, sales and advisory teams to shape the product roadmap and go-to-market strategy.

Interconnected Suite of Tools

I planned to unify a fragmented toolset into a single, coherent suite, renaming each tool to reflect its actual purpose and to enhance its performance in SERPs. I designed a shared iconographic system to visually unite them, laying the groundwork for full platform integration. However, this work was paused when Schneider Electric acquired SE Advisory Services, pending a combined software strategy.

Product Showcase

Rather than a static screenshot or a raw screen recording, I chose a fast, visually driven reveal to move through the platform's capabilities in seconds. It's a format built to stop a scroll, hold attention, and get a viewer through the whole feature set before their patience runs out — the one I reach for whenever a tool has more to show than a single image can carry, but not enough time to earn a full guided walkthrough.

Animated demos

Climate Hazards

To bring a tool to life beyond a static screenshot, I used screen recordings like these to demonstrate it in action — the same clips fed into YouTube playlists of demos and walkthrough tutorials, used to build buy-in with prospects and drive adoption once a tool was live internally. Three of the ECLR platform's report tabs, screen-recorded live, above.

AI-enabled tools, built by our experts

We recognised that one of our unique selling points or elements that separated our tools and platforms from the rest was the connection to the advisory services and the experts that built the tools based on years of experience, so we ensured our team were kept visible in our content and communications. We also know from experience that our content with smiling faces in performs best, because people respond to people. It's just basic psychology!

SEED brochure design

Modernisation Representations

While CRaFT was undergoing an update to modernise its interface, the real screens weren't ready to show. I recreated representative screenshots and graphs instead — visuals accurate enough to stand in for the live product and keep marketing materials moving without waiting on the rebuild.
