Climate Risk Tool (ECLR)

A platform that analyses how vulnerable an organisation's physical sites are to climate hazards, and supports the identification and prioritisation of adaptation measures. Content, structure and go-to-market messaging developed during my nearly seven years leading marketing for EcoAct / SE Advisory Services.

What it is
ECLR
hero screenshot

With the pathway to 1.5°C becoming progressively more challenging, the need for businesses to identify, mitigate and adapt to the vulnerabilities created by climate change is becoming increasingly important. The platform lets users explore different criticalities, climate scenarios and time horizons, modelling both classical climate risks — floods, heatwaves — and less common ones, such as glacial lake outburst floods, salinisation of coastal water tables, and the impact of ocean acidification.

What the tool does

Improved climate risk management

Designed to combine scientific rigour with simplicity and flexibility, giving a complete and precise view of several climate-related aspects at once — modelling both classical hazards, like floods and heatwaves, and less common ones.

Personalised visualisation of the exposure of your sites to climate risks

Filters sites by typology, operational unit and vulnerability, taking existing adaptation actions already in place into account. Once configured for the most relevant risks, the platform visually models the most vulnerable sites; a climatology team checks the data quality and presents the results.

Understanding of current and future climate issues

Scenario analysis models climate change over time to understand the level of frequency and intensity of each risk, so adaptation actions can be matched to how frequent or intense a given risk actually is.

Defining adaptation solutions

Helps operational managers take ownership of climate issues and choose the actions best suited to their organisation, with downloadable factsheets on each site's climate risk profile.

Training and autonomy

Customers are trained to use the platform themselves and can license it to manage their own climate risks going forward — useful when expanding scope to a new site or catchment area.

Identifying the most vulnerable sites

A climatology team checks the quality of the underlying data before results are presented, so the sites flagged as most vulnerable can be trusted and acted on.

Which other key features does ECLR offer?

How does the platform identify a company's most vulnerable sites?

After configuring the platform for the risks most relevant to the business, ECLR visually models the most vulnerable sites. A team of climatologists then checks the quality of the data before the results are presented.

How does ECLR support climate adaptation strategies?

To improve the resilience of a company's infrastructure, ECLR helps operational managers get to grips with the climate issues they face, supported in choosing the actions best suited to their organisation. The platform also allows factsheets on the climate risk profile of each site to be downloaded.

How can I use ECLR?

Customers are trained in using ECLR and can purchase a licence to manage climate risks effectively themselves — a good fit for an agile approach to climate change, whether working within existing operations or expanding geographical scope, such as adding a new site or catchment area.
